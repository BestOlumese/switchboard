# F08 — Lightweight customer CRM

**Phase:** P4 · **Owner:** B (UI), A (API)

**Story:** As an owner, I know who my regulars are and who keeps not showing up.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Customer created automatically on first message/booking (by phone, E.164)
- [ ] List with search (name/phone), sort by last visit, visits, no-shows
- [ ] Detail: booking history, deposits, notes, tags (VIP, Late, Prefers Dayo)
- [ ] Owner option per business: "Require full prepayment for customers with ≥ 2 no-shows"
- [ ] Phone numbers masked to last 4 digits for `staff` role
